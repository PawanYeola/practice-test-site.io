Best Year For Gamers (2026)

  • Crimson Desert

    • Crimson Desert is an open-world action RPG set in a vast medieval fantasy world. Players explore beautiful landscapes while facing dangerous monsters, enemies, and large-scale battles. The game combines exploration, intense combat, and a detailed fantasy setting with highly impressive graphics.

  • Death Stranding 2: On the Beach

    • Death Stranding 2 is a story-driven action game developed by Hideo Kojima. It takes players on a mysterious journey through a vast and unusual world, combining exploration, survival, and cinematic storytelling. The game is known for its impressive visuals, atmospheric environments, and unique gameplay mechanics.

  • Resident Evil Requiem

    • Resident Evil Requiem is a survival-horror game that focuses on exploration, suspense, and terrifying encounters. Players must investigate dangerous locations while dealing with mysterious threats and limited resources. The game aims to deliver an intense horror experience through atmospheric environments, frightening enemies, and cinematic storytelling.

  • 007: First Light

    • 007: First Light is an action-adventure game based on the iconic James Bond character. Developed by IO Interactive, the creators of the Hitman series, the game follows a younger version of Bond and explores his early career as a spy. It combines stealth, action, espionage, and cinematic missions in the style of a modern spy thriller.

  • GTA VI

    • Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) is an upcoming open-world action-adventure game from Rockstar Games. It is set in a modern environment and is expected to feature a huge open world filled with cities, highways, vehicles, missions, and various activities. The game is one of the most highly anticipated titles in the gaming industry because of its ambitious world, advanced visuals, and immersive gameplay.

  • Assassin's Creed Black Flag: Resynced

    • Assassin's Creed Black Flag: Resynced is a pirate-themed action-adventure game featuring naval exploration, sea battles, and hidden treasures. Players can explore a large open world, travel across the ocean, and engage in exciting combat. The game focuses on adventure, exploration, and the life of a pirate.
